This research article proposes a context-aware attention mechanism for image caption generation. Standard attention-based captioners attend to image regions independently of the language context produced so far; the proposed mechanism conditions visual attention on the previously generated tokens, encouraging captions that are more semantically grounded in the image and less prone to context drift or hallucinated content.

**Published as a Research Article in *Heliyon*, Volume 10, Issue 17 — September 15, 2024.**
